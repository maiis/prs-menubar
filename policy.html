Privacy Policy for PRs MenuBar

Effective Date: 2026-09-23

PRs MenuBar ("we", "our", or "app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, where it is kept, and which servers it talks to. In short: the app has no servers of its own, and it has no analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.

1. Information the App Accesses

PRs MenuBar connects to the Git hosting services you add: GitHub, GitLab (gitlab.com or a self-hosted instance), and Gitea / Forgejo (self-hosted). Using the access token you provide, it retrieves the pull requests (or merge requests) awaiting your review, including:

  • Title, number, link, repository, and draft status
  • Author username and profile picture address
  • Creation and update dates
  • Labels and their colors

The app does not access anything beyond what is needed to show these pull requests.

2. Where Your Data Is Kept

  • Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • Account settings (account name, provider, server address) and display preferences are stored in the app's local preferences on your Mac.
  • Pull request data is held in memory only while the app runs and is never written to disk.
  • Profile pictures are cached in the app's local cache folder so they aren't downloaded again on every refresh. The cache is removed when you uninstall the app, and macOS may clear it at any time.

3. Network Connections

The app only connects to:

  • The Git servers you configure. Your token is sent only to the server of the account it belongs to. Requests identify the app by name and version.
  • Profile picture hosts. Author pictures are loaded from the addresses your Git provider returns. These are usually the provider's own servers, but can be third-party avatar services such as Gravatar, which GitLab and Gitea may use. These requests never include your token, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to that host. You can turn this off with Settings → Display → Show Author Avatars.

Links you open from the app (a pull request, or the links in the About tab) open in your default web browser.

4. Sharing Your Data

  • We do not collect, sell, trade, or share any of your data
  • We do not operate any server that receives your token or your data
  • All requests go directly from your Mac to the servers listed above

5. Your Choices

  • Remove an account in Settings → Accounts. This also deletes its token from the Keychain.
  • Turn off author pictures in Settings → Display.
  • Revoke a token at any time from your Git provider's settings (for example, GitHub or GitLab).
  • Delete all app data by removing your accounts and uninstalling the app.
